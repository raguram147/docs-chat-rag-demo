# Shipping Policy

Parcelwise is a fictional courier software company used as sample data for this demo.

## Delivery times

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days within the same country. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and is available for parcels under 20 kg. International delivery takes 7 to 14 business days depending on customs clearance.

## Shipping costs

Standard delivery costs a flat 4.50 USD for parcels up to 5 kg. Each additional kilogram costs 0.80 USD. Express delivery costs 12 USD for parcels up to 5 kg. Orders above 75 USD qualify for free standard delivery.

## Cut-off times

Orders placed before 2:00 PM local warehouse time are dispatched the same business day. Orders placed after the cut-off, or on weekends and public holidays, are dispatched on the next business day.

## Tracking

Every parcel receives a tracking number by email within one hour of dispatch. Tracking updates appear in the customer dashboard and through the Tracking API. If a tracking number shows no movement for 72 hours, contact support so the parcel can be traced with the carrier.

## Restricted items

Parcelwise does not ship lithium batteries above 100 Wh, aerosols, perishable food, or cash. Parcels containing restricted items are returned to the sender at the sender's cost.
