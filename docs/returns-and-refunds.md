# Returns and Refunds

## Return window

Customers can return an item within 30 days of the delivery date. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Clearance items marked "final sale" cannot be returned.

## How to start a return

Open the customer dashboard, choose the order, and select "Start a return". A prepaid return label is generated as a PDF. Attach the label to the parcel and drop it at any partner collection point. Return labels expire 10 days after they are generated.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned parcel is scanned at the warehouse. Card refunds can take a further 3 to 7 business days to appear on a statement, depending on the bank.

## Return shipping fees

Returns are free when the item arrived damaged or the wrong item was sent. For any other reason a return fee of 3 USD is deducted from the refund.

## Damaged or lost parcels

Report a damaged parcel within 7 days of delivery and include two photos: one of the outer packaging and one of the item. A parcel is treated as lost when tracking shows no movement for 10 business days. Lost parcels are refunded in full or re-sent at no charge, whichever the customer prefers.

## Exchanges

Parcelwise does not process direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the replacement.
